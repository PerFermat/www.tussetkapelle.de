Content and copyright

The texts and photographs on this website come from the archive of the Weber family. The accounts are attributed by name: the description of the chapel on the Tussetberg and of the miraculous images by Otto Veith (Wallern), the stories of the old Tusset Chapel by Rosl Tahedl (Guthausen), and the photographic report on the reconstruction by Emil Weber. The Stations of the Cross were painted by Helma Fritsche-Flügel.
