Getting in touch

Suggestions, comments and links about the Bohemian Forest

  1. Home
  2. Contact

We, the sons of Emil Weber, are very glad to receive your suggestions and comments – especially from all Bohemian Forest Germans and their descendants who feel a connection to this website and who wish to preserve the history of the Bohemian Forest. We are happy to add links to our website that relate to this subject.

How to reach us

Simply send your suggestions to info@tussetkapelle.de
