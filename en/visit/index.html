How to reach the new Tusset Chapel

Finding us (opens in a new tab)

You reach us via the A3 motorway towards Passau, leaving at the Aicha vorm Wald exit. From there continue to Hutthurm. From Hutthurm the B12 road takes you via Freyung to Philippsreut.

At the chapel

The book on the history of the new Tusset Chapel is available in the chapel and may be purchased for 2.50 €.
